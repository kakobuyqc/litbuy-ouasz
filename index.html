Litbuy spreadsheet guide

Build a list you can
actually compare.

A useful Litbuy finds spreadsheet records enough evidence to support a decision. Keep uncertain details marked as unknown until you verify them.

  1. 01

    Save the exact product reference

    Record the product URL, seller name, selected variant and date checked. Give each entry a descriptive name instead of relying on a photo alone.

  2. 02

    Check fit and construction

    Compare measurements with an item you own. Note fabric composition, fastening type, dimensions and any missing information.

  3. 03

    Estimate the landed cost

    Keep the item price separate from delivery, service fees and applicable import charges. Check current quotes for your destination.

  4. 04

    Review before committing

    Reopen the listing, confirm availability and review the seller’s terms. A saved spreadsheet row can become outdated.

Questions, answered plainly

Before you
follow a find.

Use the guide as a starting point, then verify the details on the destination listing.

What is a Litbuy spreadsheet?

A Litbuy spreadsheet is a shopping comparison sheet organized around product links and notes for people researching Litbuy-related finds. Useful fields include category, measurements, listed price, seller details and the date checked. This page is an independent guide, not an official Litbuy service.

Does a spreadsheet verify product authenticity?

No. A spreadsheet, brand name or product photograph does not establish authenticity, seller authorization or manufacturing quality. Verify those details independently before purchasing.

How should I compare sizes?

Compare the listing’s measurements with an item you already own. For clothing, check chest, shoulder, length and waist measurements. For shoes, check the stated sizing system, size chart and available foot or insole measurements.

How do I estimate the total cost?

Add the item price, applicable domestic delivery, service fees, international shipping and any applicable taxes or duties. Currency conversion charges may also apply. Obtain current quotes before paying.

Where do the category links lead?

The category links open the corresponding CAFANS category pages. Other shopping links open the CAFANS homepage. This page does not provide checkout or a live inventory feed.

Are the pictured items available on CAFANS?

The images provide visual references only. They do not confirm CAFANS stock, product authenticity, price, brand affiliation or availability. Check the destination listing for current details.
